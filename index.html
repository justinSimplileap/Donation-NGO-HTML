Forever Cherished

A Legacy That Will Touch Many More

SARINI MEMORIAL FOUNDATION is an initiative established in loving memory of Sarita Chachan, a woman whose life was defined by empathy, generosity and an extraordinary willingness to help others.

Inspired by her values and the countless lives she touched, the foundation aspires to carry forward her legacy by creating meaningful opportunities for underprivileged children through education and healthcare.

We believe that every child deserves the chance to learn, grow, live a healthy life and realise their full potential. Through this initiative, we hope to transform the values she cherished into a lasting impact that reaches generations to come.

Our Initiatives

Educational assistance

Supporting children who face financial barriers to accessing and continuing their education.

Learning resources

Providing books, stationery, school supplies and other essential educational materials.

Scholarships and continued learning

Exploring financial assistance and scholarship opportunities for deserving students.

Guidance and mentorship

Encouraging confidence, curiosity, personal development and lifelong learning.

Essential healthcare

Facilitating access to appropriate medical care for children in need.

Preventive health

Supporting health check-ups, early identification of health concerns and preventive services.

Health awareness

Promoting awareness of hygiene, nutrition, oral health and healthy habits.

Nutritional well-being

Supporting initiatives that encourage better nutrition and healthy childhood development.
